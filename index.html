How the circuit works

Race

Black starts on the right and White on the left. Move around the outer track into the opposite home. All 15 must be there before bearing off to the bank.

Protected positions

Two or more same-color bricks cannot be hit or jailed. A wall of the configured size also blocks an opponent from passing through.

Two dice

Use different dice separately, or combine both before using either. Doubles create four equal units; group them freely.

Jail mode

A lone opponent is trapped until its jailer moves. A lone jailer may itself be jailed; its prisoner then escapes. Jailing on enemy-home position 1 awards two round points.

Bearing off

The furthest remaining brick has priority. Clear deeper bricks before collecting nearer ones.

Controls

Roll, hover or tap a movable brick, then choose a highlighted point. Explicit move buttons resolve grouped dice choices.
